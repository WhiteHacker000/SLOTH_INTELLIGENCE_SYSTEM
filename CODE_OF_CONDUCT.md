# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Be considerate in discussions and reviews. Critique ideas and code, not people. If someone asks you to stop a behavior, stop.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, including removing content or restricting participation. Please do not disclose another person's private information in a report unless it is necessary to address the concern.
