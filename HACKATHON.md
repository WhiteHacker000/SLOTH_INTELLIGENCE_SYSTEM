**Selected Problem Statement:06** 
Open-Source Decision Intelligence and Knowledge Recovery

**Problem Overview:**
Open-source projects accumulate years of technical discussions across GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, and documentation. Critical architectural decisions, their reasoning, and rejected alternatives often remain undocumented, making historical knowledge difficult to recover for new contributors.

**Why we are using Qwen3-8B-Instruct?**

Purpose: Transform retrieved discussions into understandable, evidence-based explanations.
We propose an AI-powered Decision Intelligence and Knowledge Recovery System that retrieves historical discussions, identifies technical decisions, and explains the reasoning behind them.
